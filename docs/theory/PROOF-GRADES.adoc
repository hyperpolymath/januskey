= Proof Witness Grades: receipts, lineage and review
:toc: preamble
:icons: font

This page backs the Proof Witness Grade records in `PROOF-NEEDS.adoc`
(§Proof Witness Grades). It answers the question a reader of the ledger should
not have to reconstruct: *which claims are proofs, how many independent
witnesses each has, and what the receipts say.* It also records a design
review of what each graded theorem actually establishes.

No percentage appears here, and none is introduced. Grades are reported as a
census with their denominator.

== Census

*`PWG-3` 5 (G-1, T-1, T-2, T-3, E-1m) · `PWG-2i` 0 · `PWG-2ii` 0 · `PWG-1` 0 ·
`ungraded` 3 (G-3, E-1, "every operation is reversible") · of 8.*

None of the five `PWG-3` rows rests on an axiom. A `PWG-3` grade is not a
discharge: each of the five still owes its Rocq and Lean legs.

The three `ungraded` rows are `OPEN`. G-3 (the FFI bridge preserves grade 𝟙)

about an implementation under cryptographic assumptions (X-1). Its model-level

== The `PWG-3` receipt

A receipt is the log of the `agda-proofs.yml` job (*Reversibility as a grade
(Loss instance), expected rejections*) on the exact source commit. From this
change on, `scripts/check-agda.sh` prints every §8.2 field into that log:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Field | Where it comes from

| Checker and version | `agda --version`. CI pins `agda-bin=2.6.4.3-1+b2` on
  `debian:trixie-slim@sha256:a29215f6…` and fails unless the version string is
  exactly `Agda version 2.6.4.3`.
| Flags in force | `flags:` line:
  `--safe --without-K --cubical-compatible --no-libraries --ignore-interfaces --double-check`
  plus the two include paths. This is §2.1's canonical form with three
  tightenings: no library is resolved implicitly, the sibling is re-checked
  from source rather than trusted from compiled interfaces, and the checker
  re-verifies its own work.
| Standard library | `standard-library: none (--no-libraries)`. The modules
  import only `Agda.Builtin.*` and the pinned sibling.
| Sibling pin | `sibling:` line: `hyperpolymath/resource-algebra`
  `cf9235c532712a783a404c359a1055f40134794a`, fetched by
  `scripts/fetch-siblings.sh`.
| Import cone | `cone(<module>):` lines, from Agda's own `--dependency-graph`.
  At this writing:
  `Reversibility` → `ResourceAlgebra.{Core,Instances.Loss,Prelude}` plus
  builtins; `TransactionAtomicity` → `ResourceAlgebra.Prelude` plus builtins;
  `Epistemic` → builtins only.
| Axioms | `--safe`, which refuses `postulate` and the unsafe pragmas in every
  module checked; and `escape-hatch-scan:`, a comment-aware scan of every
  cone file the checker does not ship (3 first-party, 3 sibling) for
  `postulate`, trust primitives, check-disabling pragmas and unsafe
  options. The scanner proves itself on every run against one clean control
  and four planted hatches, so a scanner that stopped matching would fail
  the gate. `Agda.Primitive` and `Agda.Builtin.*` bind the checker's
  BUILTINs. They ship with the pinned checker and are attributed to it.
| Input digest | `input:` lines (SHA-256 per scanned file) and `input-digest:`
  over them.
| Source commit | `source:` line, the full 40-character commit.
| Result | The three modules type-check, and each fixture under
  `tests/reject-agda/` is refused with the message it names.
|===

Caveat, not an axiom: Agda reports `UnsupportedIndexedMatch` warnings (14 in
`Reversibility`, 16 in `TransactionAtomicity`, 2 in `Epistemic`, including the
sibling's `≤-trans`). These clauses would not compute under cubical
transports. That is a statement about computation, not about the propositions,
so the gate leaves the warnings visible.

== Design review of the graded theorems (2026-11-09)

A grade counts witnesses. It does not measure how much a theorem says. This
review records what each graded row actually establishes, so that a reader
does not take a `PWG-3` statement for more than it is.

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Row and theorem | Kind | What it establishes, and its limits

| G-1 `all-lossless-trace`, `lossless-trace-all`, `obliterate-absorbs`,
  `obliterated-not-lossless`
| Substantive (induction over traces)
| The grade algebra: a trace is lossless exactly when every step is, and one
  obliterating step makes the whole trace `inf`. Grades are annotations on
  steps. Nothing derives a step's grade from its code.

| G-1 `wrap-unwrap-𝟙`, `wrap-unwrap-id`
| Definitional or premise-restating
| `wrap-unwrap-𝟙` is `refl`, because both steps are annotated `fin 0`.
  `wrap-unwrap-id` returns the record's own `cancels` premise. Their content
  is the premise, which is TESTED as `execute_then_undo_is_identity`.

| T-1 `rollback-restores`
| Substantive (induction over executed lists)
| Undoing a fully executed list in reverse restores the start state, given
  each step's undo law (T-6, ASSUMED). `ForwardRollback.agda` pins that
  execution-order undo is refused.

| T-2 `lifecycle-preserves-good`, T-3 `at-most-one-active`
| Substantive (case analysis and list lemmas)
| The four transitions keep `Good`, rewrite only the Active transaction, and
  leave at most one Active. The model matches the Rust by transcription (T-11,
  ASSUMED). The premise `Good` is now checked when a log is loaded (T-10,
  TESTED). Before 2026-11-09 a hand-edited or foreign log could start the
  Rust outside the invariant, and the unused `TransactionManager::active_mut`
  could change a transaction outside the four transitions. That method has
  been removed.

| E-1m `adversary-ignorant`, `obliterate-ignorance`
| Witness-specific
| Proved for two particular worlds whose stores differ and whose adversary
  observations are both empty. The general form is
  `different-store-unknown`, which needs such a pair as its hypothesis. These
  statements show that ignorance is expressible and true in the model. They
  do not show that the adversary is ignorant in every januskey state.

| E-1m `obliterate-cuts-recovery`
| Substantive (small)
| After obliterating `h`, the model store holds nothing at `h`.

| E-1m `rotate-forgets-old`
| Definitional
| Holds because the model's `rotate` sets the old key unusable for every
  agent. `OldKeyStillUsable.agda` pins that the opposite is refused, but no
  link exists to the Rust key subsystem.
|===

No row was found false. The two corrections that follow from this review are
made in this change: rows T-10 and T-11 now exist, and the narrower reading
of E-1m above is recorded. They do not change any grade.

== Not graded, and why

=== Idris2 ABI rows (engineering)

A-1, A-2 and C-1 to C-5 are `PROVEN` Idris2 theorems, checked by
`idris2 --typecheck src/abi/januskey-abi.ipkg` (in `idris-abi.yml` and
`dashboard-check`). §8.1 does not grade engineering proofs. G-2 (Idris2
`IsCNO`/`ReversibleOp` against Agda `ReversiblePair`) is `ASSUMED`, by
transcription.

=== Rust obligations (Creusot, Pearlite and Verus)

Every `OPEN` here has the same reason. No Creusot or Verus toolchain is set up
in this repository or its CI, and the agent sandboxes that wrote these rows
could not install one (`docs/estate/environment-toolchains.md`). Next action:
pick the smallest obligation, T-10's invariant check, write it as a Pearlite
contract and a Verus specification, and add a pinned CI job for each. File I/O
(T-5, T-8, S-2) sits at the edge of what either verifier models, so a
contract there covers the in-memory logic, with the filesystem as an
`ASSUMED` boundary.

=== Other rows

T-6, T-7, T-11, X-1 and G-2 are `ASSUMED`. G-3, E-1, T-9, L-1, L-2 and O-1 are
`OPEN`. V-1 to V-3 are `DESIGNED`. Each row names its own gap in
`PROOF-NEEDS.adoc`, and none may borrow the grade of a neighbouring row: "a
row is only as strong as its weakest step" (README-EXPLAINME).

== The epistemic rewrite: E-1 becomes E-1m

januskey's Idris2 ABI carries `Knows : Agent → Type → Type` (`Types.idr`,
§Level 12). It could not state the security claims the ledger attributed to it:
`Knows Adversary a` is uninhabited *by construction*, and "the system forgets
the old key" was unstateable, because `Knows` has no negative form.
`verification/proofs/agda/Epistemic.agda` replaces it with knowledge as
indiscriminability over an observation, so that ignorance is a provable
proposition (`¬ Knows`). Its theorems, and what they do not establish, are in
the design review above. E-1 stays `OPEN`. The Idris module is unchanged,
because the theory belongs in Agda (§1) and the ABI in Idris2.

== Reporting rules this repository follows

* No percentage for a proof claim, ever. The TOPOLOGY bars are project-plan
  measures, not proof measures.
* Every grade is derived from a named receipt, and a receipt names its commit,
  checker version, flags, library set, sibling pin, import cone, axiom scan
  and inputs.
* A change to a graded artefact, its checker or its pin makes the next run the
  receipt. Older receipts are kept with their lineage state and never deleted.
* Always write the `PWG-` prefix. A bare grade number collides with CRG, TRG,
  ARG and FRG, and with ECHIDNA's `TrustLevel`, which counts the other way.
